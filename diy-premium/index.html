Good to know

A few calm answers.

What’s included in the kit estimate?

Your estimate above covers the kit items quoted for your sizes. Rock, delivery, equipment hire, engineering and council fees are separate unless your quote specifically includes them. We’ll confirm inclusions before you order.

Do I need engineering or council approval?

Often yes — it depends on wall height, site conditions and your local requirements. On a call we can talk through what typically applies and what drawings may be needed. Approvals are not guaranteed by us; your site and council decide.

Can gabion sit in front of an existing limestone wall?

Sometimes — as a screening or feature approach after a proper site assessment. It is not a repair method for an unsafe or failing wall. If the existing wall needs structural attention, that comes first.

How long does a DIY build take?

It varies with length, height, access, rock handling and weather. Some homeowners stage the work over weekends; others need longer. We’ll help you plan a realistic sequence rather than promise a fixed timeframe.

What about the mesh?

Our DIY kits use Galfan-coated mesh cages, supplied flat-packed for assembly. Coatings and product certifications relate to the mesh system — not an unconditional lifespan for every finished wall on every site.

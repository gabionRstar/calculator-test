A few things you might want to know before getting started

We’ve answered some common questions to help you decide whether a DIY gabion wall is right for your project.

Do I need engineering for my gabion wall?

Not every gabion project requires individually prepared engineering drawings. Requirements depend on wall height, site conditions, soil, slopes and any additional loads. Gabion Fabrication can arrange engineering on request where required. Our kits are supplied as DIY products and do not automatically include site-specific engineering.

Do I need council approval?

In WA, some retaining walls up to 500 mm high may be exempt from a building permit, provided they meet the relevant exemption conditions. Taller walls and certain site situations may require approval. Building-permit exemptions do not necessarily remove planning approval requirements, so check with your local council before construction.

Do I need building experience to install a gabion wall?

Gabion cages are assembled with connectors such as spirals or clips (depending on your kit), filled with stone and secured with lids. Our installation guidance explains the process step by step. You don’t need traditional bricklaying skills, although suitable preparation, tools and physical work are required. Retaining walls must be constructed to the appropriate design and site requirements.

Are gabions cheaper than limestone retaining walls?

Doing the work yourself offers potential savings on installation labour. However, the overall cost depends on wall dimensions, stone, delivery, equipment and site preparation. Your estimate gives you a starting point for comparing the DIY gabion kit with a comparable limestone wall. Remember to compare equivalent scopes, including materials, delivery and installation.

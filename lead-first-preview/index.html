A different approach

Have you considered gabions instead?

Traditional retaining walls can involve significant installation costs. Gabion walls offer a different approach: a steel mesh cage system filled with natural stone.

  • No traditional bricklaying required
  • Choose a suitable stone and finish you like
  • Assemble and fill the cages yourself
  • Potentially save on installation labour

Savings depend on your site, stone, equipment and how much of the work you take on.

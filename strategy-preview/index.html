Good to know

Questions before you get started

Do I need building experience?

No specialist gabion-building experience is assumed. The cages are supplied flat-packed for assembly, and our DIY guide explains the general process. You should be comfortable with the physical work involved, including handling heavy stone. If you would rather not do the installation yourself, you can arrange your own contractor.

Does the kit include the stone?

Your estimate will show whether a rock allowance has been included. Rock selection, quantity, availability and delivery costs need to be confirmed for your project. You can choose a suitable stone finish to complement your home.

Does the kit include everything I need?

Your final quote will list the cage sizes, mesh, connectors, bracing and other components included. Site preparation, excavation, base materials, drainage, rock-handling equipment, engineering, permits and installation labour are not included unless specifically stated.

Do I need engineering or council approval?

Retaining-wall approval requirements depend on the wall’s height, location, site conditions and relationship to adjoining land or other structures. In WA, walls over 500 mm commonly require a building permit, but other circumstances can also trigger approval requirements. Check with your local government and get site-specific professional advice before construction. We can discuss engineering options, but a kit estimate and general guide are not a site-specific design or approval.

Can I build over an existing limestone wall?

Sometimes a gabion feature can be used as a visual screen, but it must not be treated as a repair for an unsafe or failing retaining wall. The existing structure and site need to be assessed before deciding whether this is suitable.

How long does installation take?

There is no single timeframe. It depends on wall size, access, preparation, stone handling, equipment and how much time you can dedicate to the project. We can help you understand the sequence so you can plan realistically.

Can I get help before ordering?

Yes. Send us your dimensions and project details and we’ll help clarify the kit configuration, inclusions and next steps. Engineering can be discussed separately where required.

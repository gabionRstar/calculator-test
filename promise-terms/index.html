TWO-DAY QUOTE PROMISE
Your quote in 2 days or we pay you $500.*

Promise terms

Last updated 18 October 2026

We want you to have your price fast. If we miss our two-day deadline, we'll pay you $500 when you go ahead with us. Here's how it works, in plain English.

1. The promise

If you send us a complete enquiry and we don't send you a written quote within 2 business days, we'll pay you $500, as long as you then accept that quote.

2. When the clock starts

  • Business days are Monday to Friday, not counting WA public holidays.
  • The clock starts when we receive your complete enquiry (see section 3).
  • Enquiries received after 5pm (WA time), on a weekend or on a WA public holiday start the clock on the next business day.
  • If we need more information from you, the clock starts once we have it.
  • If we tell you a site visit is needed before we can price your job, the clock starts at the end of the day of the site visit.

3. What's a complete enquiry

To start the clock, we need:

  • your name, phone number and email
  • the site suburb or address
  • the approximate wall length and height
  • the wall type you're after (gabion or limestone, or "not sure").

A site photo isn't required, but it helps us quote faster.

4. What counts as a quote

A written quote or an itemised written estimate, sent to you by email or SMS. It counts as sent when it leaves us, even if you read it later.

5. Who it applies to

  • Residential retaining wall projects in the Perth and Bunbury regions.
  • One claim per property and per project.
  • It's for the person who made the enquiry and can't be transferred.
  • It can't be combined with any other offer or discount.

6. When it doesn't apply

  • We can't reach you because the contact details you gave us are wrong, or you don't reply to our questions.
  • You ask us to hold off or change the job's scope (the clock restarts from your change).
  • The job needs an engineer's input or council approval before it can be priced.
  • Events outside our reasonable control, such as extreme weather, natural disasters or major system outages, stop us from quoting on time.

7. How to claim

  • Let us know within 14 days after the 2-business-day deadline has passed.
  • Accept the quote within its validity period (30 days from the quote date, unless your quote says otherwise).

8. How we pay it

The $500 only applies once you accept the quote. At our choice, we'll either:

  • transfer $500 to you within 14 days of your deposit being paid, or
  • reduce your accepted quote amount by $500, shown as a credit on your invoice.

It isn't paid as cash, and isn't paid at all if the quote isn't accepted.

9. The fine print

  • We may change or end this promise for new enquiries at any time. Any change won't affect enquiries we've already received.
  • Nothing in these terms limits your rights under the Australian Consumer Law.
